# Imperial 3-Bet Membership — staging proposal

Price: **$120/month**. Status: configured in staging only, no purchases or token issues.

Benefits:
- Monthly IGN benefit targeting **$80** in value, **no token quantity yet** because there is no verified audited market quote, issuance model, redemption/custody rules or legal approval. **Do not market this as a guaranteed monetary asset.**
- **200 educational hand-analysis tokens (credits) each membership month**. One credit is intended to unlock one *completed, owner-accessible hand* review from the Past Played Hands directory only. No live-hand AI assistance; AI engine still pending.
- **Six 12-hour reward boosters** per membership month, with no automatic rollovers. Reward multiplier and eligibility milestones are deliberately not set yet; boosters must not run absent a funded, capped, abuse-resistant reward pool.
- Draft 3-Bet Club-style **fee rakeback**: 10% base, 12.5% intermediate, 15% top level subject to specific qualification requirements. Distinguish rake actually paid from *lost wagers*; don't incentivize chasing losses.
- Potential tournament entry savings are pending defined limits and approval. Never assume third-party branding/affiliation.

Accounting/release blockers:
- Safe, idempotent paid subscription renewal handling, cancellations/refunds, chargeback reversal, PCI/PSP rules.
- Entitlement ledger and credit expiration/rollover definitions, protected 200-credit monthly issuance, 6 nonoverlapping or explicitly stacked boosts, usage receipts, renewal boundary and fraud tests.
- Membership authentication, age/jurisdiction/KYC checks, responsible-gambling exclusions, wager-loss rebate review, caps and reserves.
- Immutable per-hand history from server settlement and post-hand study integration.
- IGN amount & exchange methodology must be expressly approved; balance types cannot silently become tradable/redeemable.
- Third-party 3-Bet Club features are design inspiration, not partnership or trademark license.

No real-money gaming, payout, or membership subscription activated.
